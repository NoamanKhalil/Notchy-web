# Copy text from the screen on a Mac

To copy text that is on the Mac screen, open the notch and choose Copy Text. It reads the type on the screen. The preview on this site does not.

## Text that is already on the screen
"Copy text from the screen on a Mac," "extract text from a screenshot," and "OCR on Mac" are the same job. The words are in a window, a paused video, or a picture, and a normal copy does nothing because the type is not selectable.

Notchy's Copy Text is for that. It reads type that is on the screen. It sits in the tools row of the notch panel, with the focus timer, the drop shelf, the color picker, and Scan QR.

## From the notch
Rest the pointer on the notch, or click it, and the panel drops down. Copy Text is in the tools row. On your Mac it reads the type on the screen. The button on this website says so, then stops. The preview does not read the page you are looking at.

It is not a screenshot. Screen, Area, and Scroll save a picture. Copy Text is the words. /blog/tools/ Tools in the notch panel lists the rest of that row, and /blog/screenshots/ Screenshots from the notch is the picture.
