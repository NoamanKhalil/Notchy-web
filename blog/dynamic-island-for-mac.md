# Dynamic Island for Mac

A Dynamic Island for Mac is a notch app that behaves like the iPhone island, usually for music or whatever is happening now. Alcove is built for that. Notchy uses the notch for a screenshot and a recording.

## What the phrase means
People search "Dynamic Island for Mac" when they want the black cutout on a MacBook to wake up the way the island does on an iPhone. Music. A timer. Something that just happened.

That is a real group of apps. https://tryalcove.com/ Alcove calls itself a Dynamic Island for the Mac. https://getnotchnook.com/ NotchNook and https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch Boring Notch put music, a calendar, and a file tray in the same spot. /blog/other-notch-apps/ What else lives in the notch goes through those three.

## Not the same search as a screenshot
Notchy is not that island. There is no now-playing control and no visualizer. A glow brightens as the pointer gets close. Rest on the notch, or click it, and the panel drops down with your shortcuts, a screenshot, and a recording.

If the phrase you typed was the iPhone island, start with the app that says so on its own site. If the phrase you wanted was a shot or a recording from the top of the screen, this is the panel for that.
