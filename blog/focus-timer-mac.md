# A focus timer on the Mac

People search for a Pomodoro timer or a menu bar timer on the Mac. Notchy's focus timer starts at 25 minutes, in the notch panel, next to the screenshot and the recording.

## Pomodoro, menu bar timer, focus timer
Those three phrases are one job: a block of time, then a stop. Notchy's block starts at 25 minutes. In the tools row the button reads "25 min" until it is running, then it counts down.

It does not keep a history, and it does not schedule the break after the block. It is the 25 minutes.

## It sits in the notch
You do not keep a separate timer in the Dock. Open the notch and it is in the tools row, beside Shelf, Copy Text, Color, and Scan QR. Starting it does not take a screenshot. The capture buttons stay next to it.

/blog/tools/ Tools in the notch panel lists the rest of that row, including the ones you can leave off.
