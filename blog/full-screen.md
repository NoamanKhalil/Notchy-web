# Full-screen apps and the glow

When an app is full screen, Notchy keeps the glow off so it does not cover that app's menus. Click the notch to open the panel.

## Why it stays off
A full-screen app puts its own menus along the top. A glow there would get in the way. Notchy leaves the glow off for that case.

The panel is still there. Click the notch and it drops down. Resting the pointer on the notch does not open it while the glow is off.

## Try it on this page
The home page has a Full-screen app switch under the preview. Turn it on and the glow stops tracking the pointer. Click the notch and the panel still opens. Turn the switch off and the glow comes back.
