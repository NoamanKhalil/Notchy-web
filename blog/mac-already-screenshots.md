# The Mac already takes the shot

Shift Command 3, 4 and 5 stay the Mac's own screenshot keys. Notchy does not turn them off. It adds the notch, a scrolling page, and a recording with a timer.

## The keys Apple already gave you
You do not need another app to take a screenshot. Shift Command 3 takes the whole screen. Shift Command 4 takes an area, and the Space bar turns that into a window. Shift Command 5 opens the Mac's own screenshot panel, which can also start a recording.

Those keys stay yours. Notchy does not replace them and does not turn them off.

## What the notch adds
Notchy puts the same kind of work on the notch, with its own chords so the two sets do not collide. Ctrl Opt Cmd 3 , 4 and 7 are the screen, an area, and a long scrolling page. Ctrl Opt Cmd 5 and 6 start and stop a recording of the screen or an area.

The scrolling page is the one the standard keys do not offer as a button. The recording shows a timer and Stop in the panel. The glow pulses amber if the Mac still has to allow the capture, and green when it finishes. A finished shot can say it was saved to the Desktop, with Show in Finder.

A dedicated capture app, the kind with a full set of markup tools, is a deeper editor after the fact. Notchy is where the shot starts. /blog/screenshots/ Screenshots from the notch walks through Screen, Area and Scroll.
