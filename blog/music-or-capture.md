# Music, a file tray, or a capture

The notch apps that came first are mostly now-playing and a file tray. Notchy takes the other job: a screenshot and a recording, from the notch.

## The jobs that repeat
Read across /blog/other-notch-apps/ NotchNook, Alcove and Boring Notch and two jobs keep showing up. One is music: what is playing, and a control for it. The other is files: drag something into the notch and pick it up later. Boring Notch and NotchNook both talk about that tray. NotchNook's can sit next to a calendar and widgets. Alcove aims the same piece of screen at what is happening now, in the style of the iPhone's Dynamic Island.

A third job is capture. https://quackscreen.com/ QuackScreen is one of the apps that takes it: their site describes a short recording from the notch, capped at three minutes, on macOS 26 and Apple silicon, plus a screenshot you can drag out of the notch.

## The job Notchy takes
Notchy is that third job, without a song visualizer. Screen, Area and Scroll are the screenshots. Screen and Area are the recordings, with a timer and Stop. The keys are Ctrl Opt Cmd 3 , 4 and 7 for shots, and 5 and 6 for recordings. /blog/shortcuts/ The shortcut list is the full set.

The glow is how you know the notch noticed the pointer. It pulses amber when something needs approval, which a capture often does, because the Mac asks first. It pulses green when that thing is done.

If you want the song that is playing drawn in the notch, one of the other apps is built for that. If you want the shot and the recording without leaving the top of the screen, that is what this panel is for.
