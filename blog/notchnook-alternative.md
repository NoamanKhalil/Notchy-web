# A NotchNook alternative

NotchNook alternative, Boring Notch alternative, and Alcove alternative are the same search. Those apps are music, files, and a calendar. Notchy is the alternative when the job is a screenshot or a recording.

## Which part you actually wanted
"NotchNook alternative" shows up next to "Boring Notch alternative" and "Alcove alternative." Someone tried a notch app, or read about one, and wants another. The useful split is the job.

On its own site, NotchNook Pro is music, a calendar, files, a teleprompter, and AI agents. Classic is media controls, Calendar, widgets, and a file tray. Boring Notch describes music with a visualizer, a calendar, a file shelf that can AirDrop, and the Mac's volume and brightness display. Alcove presents itself as a Dynamic Island for the Mac.

Notchy does not play the song in the notch, and it does not try to be that island.

## When the alternative is Notchy
Notchy is the one to look at when the job is the screen. Screenshot the whole screen, an area, or a long scrolling page. Record the screen or an area, with a timer and Stop. A 25 minute focus timer, a drop shelf, copy text, a color picker, and Scan QR sit in the same panel.

If you wanted the file tray and the music controls, NotchNook or Boring Notch is the closer match. Their pages are where to read the price and the current list. This site does not sell a license.
