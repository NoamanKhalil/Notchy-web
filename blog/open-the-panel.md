# Open the panel from the notch

Rest the pointer on the notch or click it. The panel drops down with your shortcuts, screenshots, recording, tools, Settings and Quit.

## Two ways to open it
Bring the pointer onto the notch and let it rest there. The panel drops down. A click on the notch does the same thing, which is the way that still works when a full-screen app has turned the glow off.

The top of the panel is your shortcuts. Finder, Safari and Terminal are there unless you have changed them. Under that are screenshots, recording and tools. Settings and Quit sit at the corners.

## What is inside
- Shortcuts you chose. Finder, Safari and Terminal by default.
- Screenshot: the whole screen, an area, or a long scrolling page.
- Record: the whole screen or an area, with a timer and Stop.
- Tools: focus timer, drop shelf, copy text, Scan QR, color picker, keep awake, world clock, and optional camera mirror, Downloads alerts and next meeting.

The preview on the home page opens the same panel. Its buttons do not leave this page.
