# What else lives in the notch

NotchNook, Alcove and Boring Notch already live in the Mac notch. They lean on music, files and a calendar. Notchy is the glow, your shortcuts, the screenshot and the recording.

## Three apps people already name
Search for a notch app and the same names come up. They are not copies of each other. Each one picked a job.

https://getnotchnook.com/ NotchNook, from lo.cafe, calls itself the original notch app. On their site, NotchNook Pro is music, a calendar, files, a teleprompter and AI agents. Classic is media controls, Calendar, widgets and a file tray. They offer a 48-hour try, and they say notes, files and anything from the camera stay on the Mac.

https://tryalcove.com/ Alcove presents itself as a Dynamic Island for the Mac. That is the iPhone idea: the notch shows what is happening now, instead of holding a tray of files.

https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch Boring Notch is an open-source project under GPL-3.0, with a page at https://theboring.name/ theboring.name. The project describes a music control with a visualizer, a calendar, a file shelf that can AirDrop, and a replacement for the Mac's volume and brightness display.

## Where Notchy fits
Notchy is not a music player, and it is not a dashboard of widgets. A soft glow brightens as the pointer gets close to the notch. Rest there, or click, and the panel drops down.

In that panel: the shortcuts you chose, a screenshot of the whole screen, an area, or a long scrolling page, and a recording of the screen or an area with a timer and Stop. The tool row is short. It is a 25-minute focus timer, a drop shelf, copy text, a color picker and Scan QR. Keep awake, a world clock, a camera mirror, Downloads alerts and the next meeting can be turned on. They are not the front of the panel.

A place to drop a file shows up often in this group. In Notchy the shelf is one tool in that row, not the reason for the app.

This site does not name a price and does not take payment. The other apps set their own. Their pages are the place to read what they charge today.
