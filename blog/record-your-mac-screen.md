# How to record your Mac screen

To record your Mac screen, press Shift Command 5 and pick the record button. Notchy does the same job from the notch: Control Option Command 5 for the whole screen, 6 for an area.

## The way the Mac already records
"How to record my Mac screen" and "screen record on Mac" are the searches. The built-in answer is Shift Command 5. That panel has a record control for the whole screen and one for an area you select. Notchy does not turn those keys off.

## From the notch instead
Notchy puts those two choices on the notch. Record Screen and Record Area are in the panel. Ctrl Opt Cmd 5 starts and stops the screen. Ctrl Opt Cmd 6 starts and stops the area. While it runs, the control shows the time and Stop.

The glow pulses amber if the Mac still has to allow the recording, and green when it stops. /blog/screen-recording/ Record the screen from the notch is the longer note.
