# Screenshots from the notch

Three shots: the whole screen, an area, or a scrolling page. The panel buttons are Screen, Area and Scroll. The keys are Control Option Command 3, 4 and 7.

## Three shots
Screen grabs the whole screen. Area grabs the part you choose. Scroll follows a long page, the kind that does not fit in one view.

Each one is in the panel, and each one has a shortcut: CtrlOptCmd3 for the screen, CtrlOptCmd4 for an area, CtrlOptCmd7 for the scrolling page.

## After the shot
When a shot finishes, the glow pulses green. The panel can report that the screenshot was saved to the Desktop, with Show in Finder beside that line. The preview on this website shows the same words and does not write a file.

Copy text and the color picker are separate tools. They are not screenshots. /blog/tools/ Tools in the panel covers them.
