# The glow around the notch

The glow tracks distance. Farther away it is faint. Closer, it is brighter. It pulses amber when something needs approval and green when that thing is done.

## Brighter as you get closer
Move the pointer toward the notch and a soft light shows up around it. The light is not a switch. It strengthens as the pointer closes in, so you can see that Notchy noticed you before the panel opens.

On the home page preview, the line under the screen shows the glow as a percent. That number is only for the preview. The Mac app shows the light itself.

## Amber and green
The same light has two signals. It pulses amber when something needs approval. It pulses green when that thing is done. A screenshot finishing is one of those done moments. Starting a recording can be one of the approval moments, because the Mac may ask before a capture.

In a full-screen app the glow stays off, so it does not sit on top of that app's own menus. /blog/full-screen/ Full-screen apps explains the click that still opens the panel.
