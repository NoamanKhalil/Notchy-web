# Tools in the notch panel

The tools row holds a focus timer, a drop shelf, copy text, Scan QR and a color picker, plus keep awake and a world clock. Camera mirror, Downloads alerts and next meeting are optional.

## The tools
- Focus timer. The panel shows it as 25 minutes, and it counts down.
- Drop shelf. A place to leave something for a moment.
- Copy text. It reads type that is on the screen.
- Scan QR. It reads a code that is on the screen.
- Color picker. It reads the color under the pointer.
- Keep awake. It keeps the Mac from sleeping.
- World clock. Times in other places.

## Optional
Three more can be turned on: a camera mirror, Downloads alerts, and the next meeting. They are optional, so the panel does not have to show them.

Settings is where those choices live, next to Quit. The preview on the home page labels each of these and does not use your camera, your Downloads folder, or a calendar.
