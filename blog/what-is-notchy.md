# What is Notchy?

Notchy lives in the notch. A glow brightens as the pointer gets close. Rest there, or click, and a panel drops down for shortcuts, screenshots, recording and tools.

## Key points
- Notchy is a Mac app. It uses the notch at the top of the screen.
- The glow is a soft light. It gets brighter as the pointer gets closer.
- Amber means something needs approval. Green means it is done.
- The panel holds shortcuts, screenshots, recording, tools, Settings and Quit.
- The notch on this website is a preview. It does not capture your screen.

## What it does
The notch is usually a camera housing and a clock. Notchy makes it a control you can reach without hunting through menus. Bring the pointer near it and a soft light appears. Rest the pointer on it, or click it, and the panel drops down.

From that panel you can open the shortcuts you chose, take a screenshot, start a recording, or use a tool such as the focus timer. Finder, Safari and Terminal are the shortcuts at the start. You choose the rest.

## What this page is
The black screen at the top of the home page behaves like the notch: the glow tracks the pointer, and a click or a rest opens the panel. The buttons explain the same actions. They do not shoot the screen, record it, or turn a camera on.
