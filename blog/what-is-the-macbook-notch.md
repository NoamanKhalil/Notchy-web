# What is the MacBook notch for?

The MacBook notch is the black cutout around the camera. On its own it does nothing you can click. Some apps hide it. Notchy turns it into a panel for a screenshot, a recording, and a few tools.

## The cutout
"What is the MacBook notch" and "what is the notch for" are the searches from a new machine. The display runs around the camera. The black shape in the menu bar is that housing. It is not a control by itself.

A related search is "hide MacBook notch." Some utilities extend a black menu bar so the cutout blends in and looks gone. That gives you the menu bar you already had. It does not add a panel.

## Making it a control
A notch app puts a job in that housing. The ones people find first put music, files, or a calendar there. /blog/dynamic-island-for-mac/ Dynamic Island for Mac is the name of that search.

Notchy puts a glow there. It gets brighter as the pointer gets closer. Rest on the notch, or click it, and a panel drops down: shortcuts, a screenshot, a recording, and tools such as a 25 minute focus timer. In a full-screen app the glow stays off, and only a click opens the panel.
