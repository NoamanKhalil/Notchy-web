Glow

A light that knows how close you are.

Move toward the notch and a soft light appears around it. It gets brighter the closer the pointer gets. It pulses amber when something needs approval, and green when that thing is done.

Near

Soft, then brighter

The glow is faint at a distance and stronger as the pointer arrives. You can see Notchy notice you before the panel opens.

Amber

Needs approval

The light pulses amber when something is waiting on you. A capture the Mac still has to allow is that kind of moment.

Green

Done

The light pulses green when the thing finishes. A screenshot that has been saved is one of those.

Panel

Rest on the notch, or click it.

The panel drops down from the notch. Your shortcuts are on top. Screenshots, recording and tools are under them. Settings and Quit are at the corners.

Shortcuts

The top row is the apps you chose. Finder, Safari and Terminal are the ones that start there.

Screenshot and record

The whole screen, an area, or a long scrolling page. Record the screen or an area, with a timer and Stop.

Settings and Quit

Settings holds the shortcuts and the optional tools. Quit leaves Notchy.

Record

A timer, and Stop.

Ctrl Opt Cmd 5

The whole screen

Starts a recording of the screen. The same shortcut stops it. The panel shows the time and a Stop control.

Ctrl Opt Cmd 6

An area

Starts a recording of the area you choose, and stops it again. The amber pulse is the “needs approval” light.

Full-screen apps

The glow stays out of the way.

When an app is full screen, Notchy keeps the glow off so it does not cover that app's menus. Only a click opens the panel. The switch under the preview does the same thing on this page.

Questions

Straight answers.

Longer versions are on the blog.

What is Notchy?

A Mac app that turns the notch into shortcuts, screenshots, recording and tools.

How does the glow work?

A soft light appears as the pointer gets near, and it gets brighter the closer it gets. Amber means something needs approval. Green means it is done.

How do I open the panel?

Rest the pointer on the notch, or click it. In a full-screen app, only the click opens it.

What are the shortcuts?

Control, Option and Command, with 3, 4 and 7 for screenshots, and 5 and 6 for recordings.

Does this website capture my screen?

No. The notch on this page is a preview. It does not take a screenshot, record, or turn a camera on.
